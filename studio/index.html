Set where your files are. Either one works on its own; with both, a changed retail character can be saved as a mod.

  • Mods folder (the repo's mods): your characters. Save writes into their folders; build them as usual.
  • Game ISO: every retail fighter and assist. Change one and Save as mod puts it in the mods folder, where it replaces the retail one in game.

Animations come from Blender: make or retarget them there, Save moves to folder, then Reload here.
